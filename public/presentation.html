2. Problem & Objectives

Explain what you chose to solve and why it matters.

📌 What to Include
  • The Core Problem: 6+ crore rural citizens in Karnataka face extreme bureaucratic delays, complex English/text-heavy portals, and zero instant crop diagnostics.
  • Who is Affected: Smallholder farmers (*ರೈತರು*), rural youth, and Panchayat Development Officers (PDOs).
  • Why it Matters: Crop disease delays cause 30-40% yield loss; unaddressed village grievances take weeks without SLA transparency.
  • Key Objectives:
    • 1. Vision AI diagnosis for 15+ Karnataka crops
    • 2. Native Kannada voice navigation & speech assistant
    • 3. 4-tier automated SLA grievance escalation engine
    • 4. Real-time APMC Mandi rates across 31 districts

3. Ideas Considered & Selection

Show that the team considered alternatives before choosing a solution.

📌 What to Include
  • Main Approaches Considered:
    • Native Android App: Required device storage, Play Store downloads, slow updates.
    • WhatsApp Chatbot: Constrained UI, no audio waveform visualizer, expensive per-message API fees.
    • IVR Phone System: Voice only; cannot perform AI crop disease scanning or photo evidence.
    • GramSetu PWA (Selected): Zero-install, instant browser/QR access, full camera & audio synthesis capability.
  • Why Selected: Zero install friction, instant evaluator QR demo, real-time Firestore sync, and rich accessibility themer.

4. Engineering Solution / Architecture

Explain how your proposed solution works.

📌 What to Include
  • Architecture: 3-tier reactive web architecture (Client PWA ➔ Google Cloud Firestore ➔ Gemini AI APIs).
  • Key Technical Engines:
    • Vision Pipeline: Canvas video capture ➔ Base64 image compression ➔ Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash Vision ➔ Structured JSON diagnosis.
    • Audio Engine: Web Speech API (`webkitSpeechRecognition`) + Web Audio API `OscillatorNode` alert chimes.
    • 4-Tier SLA Pipeline: PDO (7d) ➔ EO (14d) ➔ CEO (21d) ➔ RDPR Commissioner (28d).
